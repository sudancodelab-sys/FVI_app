Present Simple

We use the present simple for habits, routines and facts.

I / You / We / They + verb
He / She / It + verb + s
She works in a bank.
They play football every Friday.
Water boils at 100°C.
ملاحظة: نضيف s إلى الفعل فقط مع he / she / it، مثل: he plays.
