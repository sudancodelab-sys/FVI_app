Adam Has Never Flown

Adam wants to be a pilot, but he has never been on a plane. Read the story, then read Adam's story after his first flight.

Talk about life experiencesAsk: Have you ever…?Say when something happened

1The story

Adam wants to be a pilot. He has read a lot of books about planes, and he has watched hundreds of videos. He knows the names of all the big airports in the world. But there is one problem. Adam has never been on a plane!

His friends ask him questions. "Have you ever flown?" "No, I haven't." "Have you ever been to an airport?" "Yes, I have. I have seen the planes from the window." Omar laughs. "A pilot who has never flown!"

Then one day, Adam's uncle calls from Port Sudan. "Adam, I have bought you a ticket. You are going to fly to Port Sudan next week!" Adam can't believe it.

Now Adam is at the airport. He has checked in, and he has found his seat by the window. The plane is moving. Adam is excited and a little nervous. He looks out of the window and smiles. "Finally," he says. "I'm flying!"

3Point of view Now it is after the flight. Adam tells when everything happened.

A

I always wanted to be a pilot. When I was a child, I read books about planes, and last year I watched hundreds of videos. But I wasn't on a plane, not once.

Last month, my uncle called from Port Sudan. "I bought you a ticket!" he said. I couldn't believe it.

Last Tuesday, I went to the airport. I checked in and found my seat by the window. When the plane took off, I looked out of the window and smiled.

Now I can say it: I have flown! And one day, I'm going to fly the plane myself.

4What did you learn?

You learned the present perfect.

We use it for experiences in our life until now, when we don't say when. When we say when, we use the past simple.

Present perfect: we don't say whenPast simple: we say when
I have seen the planes.I saw the planes yesterday.
His uncle has bought a ticket.His uncle bought it last month.
Sara has never been to Kassala.Sara went to Port Sudan in 2020.
Have you ever flown? Yes, I have.When did you fly? Last Tuesday.
have / has + past participleI have seen, he has bought, they have flown.
ever in questions, never for noHave you ever flown? I have never flown.
Some verbs change: been · flown · seenbe → been, fly → flown, see → seen, buy → bought
