Story · Lesson 7

What Did Mr. Ali Say?

A message about a test travels from student to student, and it changes on the way. Read the story, then read the exact words people said.

Report what people saidUse said and toldPass on a message correctly

1The story

On Wednesday, Mr. Ali was sick, so a new teacher, Ms. Nadia, came to class instead. At the end of the lesson, she gave the students a message from Mr. Ali. She said that there would be a short test on Sunday. She said that it would be about the stories from Units 1 and 2, and she told the students to bring a pencil.

After class, Adam called Omar, who had been absent that day. "Ms. Nadia said that the test was on Sunday," he said. "She told us that it was about Units 1 and 2." Then Omar called Huda. "Adam said that the test was on Sunday, about Unit 2."

Huda sent a message to the class group. "Omar says there's a test on Sunday about Unit 2, or maybe Monday?" Then someone wrote, "I heard that there was no test!" By Saturday night, nobody knew what was true. Some students said that the test had been canceled. Others said that it was on Monday.

On Sunday morning, Mr. Ali came back. Half of the students had pencils, and the other half looked very surprised. "What's wrong?" Mr. Ali asked. Sara explained what everyone had said. Mr. Ali laughed. "This is a perfect lesson," he said. "Today, there is no test. Today, we are going to learn reported speech!"

3Point of view Now Sara gives the exact words that people said.

S

On Wednesday, Ms. Nadia said, "There will be a short test on Sunday. It is about the stories from Units 1 and 2. Bring a pencil, please."

After class, Adam told Omar, "The test is on Sunday. It's about Units 1 and 2." And Omar told Huda, "Adam says the test is on Sunday, about Unit 2."

Huda wrote in our group, "Omar says there's a test on Sunday about Unit 2, or maybe Monday?" Then someone wrote, "There's no test!" And others wrote, "The test has been canceled!"

On Sunday, Mr. Ali laughed. "Today, there is no test. Today, we are going to learn reported speech!"

4What did you learn?

You learned reported speech.

We use it to tell other people what someone said. The verb usually moves one step back into the past.

What she saidWhat we report
"There will be a test."She said that there would be a test.
"The test is on Sunday."He said that the test was on Sunday.
"The test has been canceled."They said that the test had been canceled.
"Bring a pencil."She told us to bring a pencil.
One step back: is → was · will → wouldhas been → had been, can → could
