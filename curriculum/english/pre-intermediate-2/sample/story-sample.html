Where Were You, Adam?

Adam was not in class yesterday. Read the story in the past, then read it again as it happens today.

Talk about yesterdayAsk: Where were you?Say where you were

1The story

Yesterday was Monday. Sara and Omar were in class, but Adam wasn't there. His chair was empty. Mr. Ali was surprised, because Adam is never absent.

In the evening, Omar was on the phone with Adam. "Where were you today, Adam? Were you sick?" "No, I wasn't. I was at the hospital with my grandfather. He was sick."

"Oh no! Was it bad?" "Yes, it was. We were at the hospital all day. My mother and my uncle were there, too. The doctors were very kind."

Today Adam is back in class. His grandfather is at home now, and he is OK. "Welcome back, Adam!" Sara and Omar are happy, and Mr. Ali is happy, too.

3Point of view Now the same story happens today.

Today is Monday. Sara and I are in class, but Adam isn't here. His chair is empty. Mr. Ali is surprised, because Adam is never absent.

In the evening, I'm on the phone with Adam. "Where are you, Adam? Are you sick?" "No, I'm not. I'm at the hospital with my grandfather. He is sick."

4What did you learn?

You learned was and were.

They are the past of am, is and are. We use them for yesterday, last week, and other times before now.

NowBefore now
I am at home. · He is sick.I was at home. · He was sick.
We are at the hospital.We were at the hospital.
He isn't here. · They aren't here.He wasn't here. · They weren't here.
Is it bad? · Are you sick?Was it bad? · Were you sick?
I · he · she · it → wasI was at home. It was bad.
you · we · they → wereWe were at the hospital all day.
Not: wasn't · weren'tAdam wasn't in class. They weren't at home.
QuestionsWere you in class yesterday?Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.
