1Read the conversation

It is the first day at Future Vision Institute. Adam meets Sara at the classroom door.
AADAMHi! I'm Adam.
SSARAHello, Adam. I'm Sara. Nice to meet you.
AADAMNice to meet you, too. Are you a new student?
SSARAYes, I am. It's my first day.
AADAMIt's my first day, too! Is this the Starter class?
SSARAYes, it is. Come in.
AADAMThanks, Sara.
SSARAYou're welcome.
