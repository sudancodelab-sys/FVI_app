The Letter E

E is the most common letter in English.
It has three main sounds.

Vowel3 sounds

1The sounds of E

/e/
Short E
  • bed
  • pen
  • ten
/iː/
Long E — it says its name
  • me
  • we
  • even
/ə/
Weak E — a soft, short sound
  • open
  • problem
  • moment

2When is E silent?

At the end of a word, E is usually silent.
It often helps the vowel before it say its name.

makehometime
Grey letter = silent
USUALLY TRUE

Short words with only one vowel say the E:
me  ·  we  ·  be
