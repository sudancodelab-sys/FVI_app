Adam's Day

A short story about a normal day. Read it, answer the questions,
then read it again from Adam's point of view.

1The story

Adam lives in Khartoum. He is a student at Future Vision Institute.

Every day, Adam wakes up at six o'clock. He drinks tea with milk, and he eats bread and beans. Then he takes the bus to the institute.

His English class starts at eight. Adam likes his class. He reads, he listens, and he speaks a lot. Mr. Ali is his teacher.

At one o'clock, Adam goes home. He has lunch with his family. In the evening, he does his homework and watches football on TV. He goes to bed at ten.

4What did you learn?

You learned the present simple.

We use it for things we do every day, again and again.
